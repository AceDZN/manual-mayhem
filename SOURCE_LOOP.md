# Direct adaptation: Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes

Original implementation of its operator/manual loop: inspect a tangible device; identify its module and serial label; consult a separate manual or describe it to a human manual reader; execute the ordered procedure; receive a strike and recover when meaning is misread; stabilize all three modules and start the festival engine.

Our fiction, assets, audio, manual and procedures are original. The device is a harmless festival airship music/weather engine. Three strikes shut it down; retry restores the same mission, preserving evidence of assistance/retries. Practice has no running clock; challenge time stops while reading, pausing and consulting settings. A static `?manual=1` reader view contains general rules and the mission catalogue without live answers or sync. Solo manual flip is complete play.

Visual thesis: ivory enamel, petrol recesses, brass controls and orange carry handles in warm workshop light.
Content plan: one dominant working device, short mission strip, compact paper manual, contextual controls.
Interaction thesis: tactile quarter-turn wheels, cable snapping between sockets, gauges and musical stems settling as modules stabilize.
