# Curriculum and evidence

Manual Mayhem uses original festival-engine procedures informed by the checked-in Workbook snapshots. The launch is selected-rule practice across three grades; it does not complete any whole chapter or demonstrate retained mastery.

## Evidence boundaries

The three stages in each grade are guided, independent and changed-context transfer. A correct action sequence demonstrates success on that finite fixture. It is evidence of unaided interpretation only when the first attempt succeeds without hints, translation or retries. A retry after informative feedback is useful practice but is not first-attempt evidence. Cosmetic placement must not reveal the right answer.

Solo manual reading assesses comprehension and reviewed message selection. It does not assess speech, listening, spontaneous writing, collaborative skill or pronunciation. An optional human reader may communicate in real life, but the program cannot validate that conversation.

The grade-7 chapter-10 strand is supporting only: understanding displayed current state versus next instructions does not establish tense mastery. No second conditional is taught. Ordinary if + present followed by a will/modal/imperative result is the selected grade-9 pattern; this is not an absolute rule that English never permits will after if. No full tense-system, literary analysis, narrative/opinion writing or long-term retention claim is made.

## Authored challenge map

The tables below describe authoritative fixture values and the intended action, rather than a claim about what a learner has mastered. Each mission contains three physical families: valve bay, cable patch bay and relay deck. The manual text and procedure tables share `src/content.ts`; `src/rules.ts` owns the consequences.

| Challenge / stage | Selected sources | Intentional contrast and world outcome | Phrase-linked feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| `g7-guided` / guided | G7/11 imperatives; G7/12 functional text; G7/6 contextual support | Small starts open and large closed: close small then open large. Connect under square, close both clips, test. Left lever only before test. | First/next identify action order; action and object must both be read. Guided help is automatically assisted evidence. |
| `g7-independent` / independent | G7/11; G7/12 | Same initial appearance but large must open before small closes. Round socket replaces square; left lever is prohibited, right required. | Must specifies obligation; do not prohibits. Wrong order/socket/lever strikes and faults that module. |
| `g7-transfer` / transfer | G7/11; G7/12 | Alternate stage equipment starts small closed/large open. Open small then close large; close clips before connecting round; right lever then left before test. | New verbs, object states and event order invalidate the remembered guided sequence. Transfer begins without a solution hint. |
| `g8-guided` / guided | G8/10 present passive; G8/11 time clauses | Cooling line is the receiver, left lever its agent. Left opens cooling; right opens music. Small closes before large opens; square cable before clips/test. | The cooling line receives the action; by the left lever identifies what opens it. The lever is not what is cooled. |
| `g8-independent` / independent | G8/10; G8/11; G8/12 mediation | Identical initial switch appearance, reversed mapping: right opens cooling, left music. After reverses valves; clips must precede square connection. Reviewed relay omits flag color but preserves order and square reference. | A short relay must preserve operational facts. Inverting event order or changing the socket is a consequential error. |
| `g8-transfer` / transfer | G8/10; G8/11; G8/12 | Replacement rain engine requests only music, opened by left. Right opens cooling and is prohibited. Open small before closing large; clips precede round connection. Relay must retain only music. | Refer to the requested receiver and its agent; do not reuse a lever from the previous mission. |
| `g9-guided` / guided | G9/11 zero conditional | Small/narrow valve open gives gauge 3, closed gives 1. Open/test, close/test, open/test repeats the rule. Fan on holds relay-deck pressure at 2. | Present-result rule is checked repeatedly; it describes a device relationship rather than one promised future event. |
| `g9-independent` / independent | G9/10 past passive; G9/12 source interpretation support | Record: left relay was replaced. Separate current check: left still fails, right works. Leave left off and use right. Round socket replaced and now explicitly works; square explicitly has no signal. | Was replaced identifies past receiver; the present check determines working state. Replacement alone is never treated as proof of function. |
| `g9-transfer` / transfer | G9/10 support; G9/11 first conditional; G9/12 mediation | Current pressure 3, next test adds 2 without fan: predicted stop at 5. Start fan before test to prevent it, relays stay off. Relay must preserve before and prohibition. Square was replaced, but both sockets now work and the procedure requests round. | Will stop predicts this startup; before states prevention order. Wrong test visibly reaches 5/stopped. Correct fan-first test stays at 3. |

All physical results also receive text feedback; sound and color alone do not carry correctness. The learner can reset a faulted module and re-read the manual; a strike remains recorded. Three strikes lead to a harmless shutdown and retry.
