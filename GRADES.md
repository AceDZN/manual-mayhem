# One campaign, source grades retained internally

The user's latest instruction replaces the earlier three grade tracks with **one sequential campaign of 30 levels**. Everyone starts at level 1; there is no grade selector, grade eligibility check or grade cap. Level numbers describe game progression, not school grades. Replaying completed levels preserves the furthest unlock.

Difficulty grows through the number of active modules, longer and reversed procedures, initial-state evidence, passive receivers, maintenance records and interacting conditional rules. The optional timer does not define the ladder.

| Campaign levels | Increasing demand | Internal Workbook provenance |
|---|---|---|
|1–3|Learn one physical family at a time: valves, cable, relays|Grade 7 chapters 11–12|
|4–6|Combine two families; compare order and both-clip requirements|Grade 7 chapters 11–12|
|7–10|Combine all three; read negation, repeated toggles and already-closed state|Grade 7 chapters 11–12|
|11–20|Identify present-passive agents/receivers, preserve before/after order, select precise operator messages|Grade 8 chapters 10–12|
|21–23|Tag the receiver of past replacement; separately consult explicitly stated current working status|Grade 9 chapters 10 and 12|
|24–26|Infer repeated target readings from fixed conditional rules, including a two-valve truth map|Grade 9 chapter 11, with retained earlier reading skills|
|27–29|Predict the stated initial startup outcome and apply a prevention plan; fan direction and stop threshold vary|Grade 9 chapters 11–12|
|30|Combine repeated calibration, replacement plus current check, startup prediction and a complete relay message|Grade 7 functional reading and grade 9 chapters 10–12|

These are selected-rule mappings, not completion of whole chapters. `sourceModules` records consulted curriculum provenance; it is not by itself evidence that every listed module is directly assessed. Reviewed message selection occurs only where a level actually includes a relay question. Static partner mode supports human communication but does not record or score speaking, listening or spontaneous mediation.

See [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md) and [coverage.json](coverage.json) for exact source modules, assessed decisions and verification. The original nine fixtures remain as semantic regression references; they are not separate learner tracks.
