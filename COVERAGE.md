The grade 7 chapter 6 [follow-the-tech-steps](https://english.workbook.co.il/grade-7/chapter-6/follow-the-tech-steps) snapshot supports the technical-procedure context; it is not claimed as a chapters 10–12 primary strand. Grade 7 chapter 10 supports current-state reading only. Pressing a control is not evidence of tense mastery.

The ladder introduces one active bay at a time (1–3), pairs (4–6), full-machine ordered tasks (7–10), passive/time-clause combinations (11–20), past records plus current checks (21–23), repeated calibration (24–26), and startup prediction/prevention (27–30). Later levels reuse earlier skills. Level 30 combines these demands. The authored differences change valid actions, rather than merely adding time pressure.

Independent tests use hand-read action sequences in `docs/qa/campaign/solutions.json`, rather than generating answers from evaluator steps. Same-state contrasts reverse first action, socket, clip order, passive agent, present working relay and fan plan. Calibration checks actual measured sequences 3/1/3, 2/4/2 and 2/4/3/1. Startup checks actual safe and stopped pressures at thresholds 5 and 6. Inactive bays cannot create repair/learning evidence.

Automated first-attempt completion is finite game evidence only. Guided levels are marked assisted; hints/translations, erroneous reviewed choices and retries remain visible in evidence. The campaign stores best finite results and unlocks the next level only after engine start. Replays do not establish retained learning.

Not assessed: entire chapters, full tense systems, second conditional, punctuation production, pronunciation, live listening, spontaneous spoken mediation, free writing, broad vocabulary mastery, classroom learning gain or long-term retention. Static partner mode supports a real human exchange but does not assess it. The ordinary first-conditional fixtures use present in the if-clause and will in the result; no universal claim is made about every possible use of will after if.

Machine-readable source/evidence mappings and current verification are in [coverage.json](coverage.json). Actual commands, screenshots and limitations are in [PLAYTEST.md](PLAYTEST.md); browser completion is established separately from pure semantic tests and source review.
