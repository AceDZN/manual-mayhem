## Do not turn teaching shortcuts into false English rules

- An event in past simple does not prove that its consequence has been undone. “I lost my ticket yesterday” does not by itself mean it was found. Give an explicit current-status sentence or a clarification option. Present perfect is not selected by a single magic time word; read the time context.
- In the ordinary future conditional taught here, use if + present and a result with will/modal/imperative. Do not claim English can **never** put will after if: willingness and embedded-question uses are outside this pack. Do not teach second conditional under the cited grade-9 module, which covers zero and first.
- Passive is about agent and receiver, not reversing time or just swapping display order. Reported speech needs known speakers/addressees and a consistent reporting time.
- Articles depend on sound and discourse: “an umbrella” versus “a large umbrella”; auto-inserted a/an is not learner production.
- Correct English outside the finite game dictionary is unsupported input, not “wrong English”. Word order feedback must allow legitimate alternatives rather than pretending the finite parser is a complete grammar.
- A dropped condition, missing referent or a newer notice's unrelated facts must not be invented by the game engine or by a supposedly correct solution.
